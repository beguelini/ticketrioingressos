# Ticket Rio: privacy and legal operations

The public notices describe the store as it operates on 1 October 2026. Publishing notices and a consent interface does not by itself establish full LGPD or GDPR compliance. The business owner should keep the following items current before enabling online sales, additional integrations, or targeted international marketing.

- Confirm and maintain the controller's legal name, CNPJ, physical address, phone, and privacy contact. Assign a responsible person to receive and answer data subject requests through `atendimento@ticketrio.com.br`; document identity verification and response procedures.
- Maintain a record of processing activities, lawful bases, retention periods, deletion procedures, vendor roles, and access controls for accounts, inquiries, profile documents, orders, and support.
- Review contracts and international transfer safeguards for Vercel, Supabase, and any future payment, email, WhatsApp, analytics, and ticketing provider. Document locations and the applicable transfer mechanism under ANPD Resolution 19/2024; assess whether GDPR territorial scope and an EU representative apply.
- Keep incident response, staff access review, backup, and supplier notification procedures. Reassess the privacy notice and checkout terms when a payment provider or real booking flow is activated.
- Audit every Google Tag Manager tag before setting `VITE_GTM_ID`. The application loads that container only after consent to both measurement and advertising; Meta Pixel loads only after advertising consent. Never add another tracking script outside the same consent controls.
- If optional tracking is activated, establish a consent audit trail and periodically verify that refusal and withdrawal prevent new tracking requests; browser preference storage alone is not an organisational record.
- Define product-specific supplier, delivery, cancellation, refund, and complaint procedures before accepting money. Current checkout is informational and must remain so until the commercial and payment flow is ready.
